# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Indian farmers lose ~₹90,000 crores annually due to crop diseases. Most farmers lack access to plant pathologists. Early detection can reduce losses by 40–60%.

## Scope
Develop an AI-powered mobile/web app that identifies 38 major crop diseases from leaf photos using Transfer Learning, provides remedy suggestions, and works offline in rural areas.

## Target Users
- Small & marginal farmers
- Agricultural extension workers
- Students and researchers

## High-level Features
1. Image upload & real-time disease prediction
2. Confidence score + Grad-CAM heatmap
3. Remedy suggestions (English + Hindi)
4. Multi-crop support (14 crops, 38 diseases)
